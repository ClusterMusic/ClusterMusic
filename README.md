Cluster is designed to be a music-centric social networking app that largely groups users by geographic communities such as universities. This geographic focus seeks to embed the app into the daily social lives of users, rather than replace it. By constricting music discovery and sharing to real-world communities, Cluster connects users around local music interests, providing more engaging and relevant content to the user as well as positioning the app within social circles, increasing engagement and fostering both online engagement and real-world experiences/events related to Cluster.
 
Notably, Cluster will have an optional but competitive element to draw engagement from the real world and separate us from traditional social media. Moreover, this competitive element will allow users to organize around smaller social circles to compete against the community as a whole.

Cluster will be entirely open-source for any individual to explore with custom-built chat systems, recommendation algorithms, and more.
